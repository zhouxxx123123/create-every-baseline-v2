# Contributing

This is a proprietary, publicly visible repository. Unsolicited code, documentation, prompt, Skill, asset, or other content contributions are not accepted.

Do not submit a pull request containing substantive material unless the copyright holder has first invited the contribution in writing and both sides have completed any required contributor or copyright agreement.

Opening an issue to report a defect or suggest an idea is welcome, but it does not grant permission to use the repository's protected material and does not obligate the copyright holder to accept, implement, compensate, or keep the submission confidential. Do not include confidential information in public issues.

If an invited contribution includes third-party material, the contributor must identify its source and license and confirm that redistribution is permitted. Repository access, a submitted pull request, review comments, or acceptance of a contribution does not by itself create rights beyond the applicable written agreements and law.
