# Evidence Validation Capstone

The team has decided that a delayed review reminder may exist, but it does not know
whether the target desktop platform can schedule it reliably after the app closes.
The team also wants to know whether users understand the recovery path.

Choose the smallest evidence route for one of these uncertainties. Define what the
evidence can and cannot prove, execute only a safe local or read-only validation,
and return the result to the originating product question.
